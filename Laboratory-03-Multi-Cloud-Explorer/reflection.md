### Mission Reflection

After completing this cloud computing mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was impressed by its wide range of services and its ability to support different types of organizations, from small startup companies to large global enterprises. AWS also provides flexible and scalable solutions that can grow based on the needs of a business. Learning about AWS helped me understand why it is widely used in the cloud computing industry.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three platforms provide essential cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and security. They also support scalability, high availability, and pay-as-you-go pricing. Although the names of their services are different, they offer similar solutions that allow organizations to build, deploy, and manage applications in the cloud.

Business requirements play an important role in choosing the most appropriate cloud platform. A company must consider factors such as budget, existing technology, security requirements, scalability, and the type of applications it needs to run. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning may prefer GCP. On the other hand, AWS can be a strong choice for organizations that need a wide range of services and global infrastructure.

By acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned how important it is to understand the needs of each client before recommending a cloud platform. I learned that there is no single cloud provider that is best for every situation. A good consultant must analyze the client's requirements and match them with the strengths and services of each cloud provider.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I now have organized documentation, comparison tables, cloud service recommendations, and Linux investigation results. These projects demonstrate my understanding of AWS, Azure, GCP, and basic cloud computing concepts. Overall, this mission improved my knowledge of cloud platforms and gave me more confidence in analyzing real-world cloud computing scenarios.
